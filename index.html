॥ Ramayana — Rama's Journey

From childhood in Ayodhya to the final departure on the Sarayu — follow Lord Rama's life across the map, stop by stop.

The saffron line is Rama's journey, in order — 13 stops, 11 places, across the seven Kandas. Tap any number on the map, or any stop below, to read its story.
